The Reality of Commercial CCTV

Passive Recording Alone Doesn't Protect Your Business

Most businesses install cameras assuming they provide security. But unmonitored footage only provides a recording of what you already lost.

Passive CCTV Alone

CCTV Records What Happened

Traditional camera systems record footage quietly onto hard drives, but nobody is watching when an incident unfolds.

Nobody may be watching the footage in real time: Feeds run unwatched on a forgotten office screen.
Store employees cannot continuously watch multiple screens: Staff must prioritize customers, ringing registers, and stock.
Business owners can't monitor cameras 24/7: You cannot stay awake all night or monitor phone notifications every second.
After-hours incidents can go unnoticed: Vandalism, lot trespass, and stockroom break-ins aren't discovered until opening.
