1

You decide which cameras we monitor

2

Honest & Responsible Security

Why We Don't Sell False Promises

In an industry plagued by exaggerated marketing claims, VigilGuard believes in total clarity about what video surveillance can and cannot do.

No security company can honestly promise that cameras prevent all crime from happening or guarantee that police officers will arrive on demand. Police department dispatch policies vary across every city and county in America.

What professional video monitoring does do is provide an immediate, active layer of human vigilance. When an unauthorized prowler enters your gas station fuel depot or climbs your warehouse fence at 2:00 AM, our operators:

  • Spot and verify the threat within seconds.
  • Deliver live two-way voice warnings over on-site speakers to startle and disperse trespassers.
  • Immediately call you and your keyholders with verified, documented video evidence.

That is real, actionable security—without the false promises.
