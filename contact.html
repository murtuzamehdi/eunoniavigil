Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about connecting your existing cameras to VigilGuard.

Do I have to purchase new cameras or hardware?

No. Our core philosophy is: Why replace your cameras when you can monitor what you already have? We securely integrate with 98% of existing commercial camera systems, IP cameras, NVRs, and DVRs over standard RTSP/ONVIF protocols with zero new hardware investment.

How does the live audio talk-down work?

If your facility has existing IP horn speakers, PA amplifiers, or two-way audio cameras, our operators can speak directly over the speakers in real time. We broadcast clear, authoritative voice warnings to startle suspects and force them off premises before damage occurs.

Does video monitoring guarantee crime prevention or police response?

No. Video monitoring provides an additional layer of human vigilance, real-time alert verification, and immediate incident notification. However, it does not guarantee that crime will be prevented or that public police will arrive within a specific timeframe, as municipal police dispatch priorities are outside our control.

Are your contracts locked for years?

No long-term lock-in. We offer flexible weekly and monthly plans to reduce buyer risk. You can scale camera counts up or down as your business expands.

Can our staff still view the cameras on our own phones?

Yes. Our connection operates as an authorized stream without disabling or changing your existing mobile apps, computer clients, or local monitor outputs.

Legal Operational Notice: VigilGuard remote video monitoring services provide an additional layer of human vigilance, proactive verification, and emergency alert escalation. Video monitoring services do not promise or guarantee that monitoring prevents crime, stops criminal acts from occurring, or guarantees police or emergency response times, which are subject to local municipal police policies and dispatch availability.
